A LITTLE FOCUS. A LITTLE RHYTHM.

Follow the echo.

Color Echo is a free memory game for iPhone and iPad. Watch the colors, listen to their sounds, and play the sequence back.

How do I play?

Tap Start game and watch the sequence. When it says Your turn, tap the colors in the same order. Each completed round adds one color and plays the full sequence again. A mistake lights all four pads, plays an error sound, and starts a fresh game.

How do I change players?

Tap the player name at the top. Choose a player or enter a new name. Profiles and high scores stay on that device. Tap the trophy to see scores for each difficulty.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Solo gameplay, sounds, profiles, and scores work without an internet connection. The game has no ads, purchases, or subscriptions.

Friends and live battles (version 1.2 and later)

Open the player button, then Friends & live battles. Sign in to Apple Game Center, choose a speed and invite a friend. Both players need the live-battle version of Color Echo, an internet connection and different Game Center accounts. You can load friends who permit access, or use Apple's invitation picker. Adding friends opens Apple's invitation interface; you choose whether to send the request.

Both players watch the same sequence, then repeat it. Both must succeed before the next round adds a colour. One mistake lets the other player win by finishing correctly; two mistakes draw. A shared time limit resolves unfinished rounds. Leaving or backgrounding the app forfeits. A general connection failure ends the match without a result. Online play does not replace your local player profiles.

Sound and difficulty

Tap the speaker to turn sound on or off. Easy, Medium, Hard, and Impossible change how quickly the sequence plays. If you leave the app, tap Resume game to hear the full sequence again.
