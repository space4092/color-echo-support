Follow the echo.

Color Echo is a free memory game for iPhone and iPad. Watch the colors, listen to their sounds, and play the sequence back.

How do I play?

Tap Start game and watch the sequence. When it says Your turn, tap the colors in the same order. Each completed round adds one color and plays the full sequence again. A mistake lights all four pads, plays an error sound, and starts a fresh game.

How do I change players?

Tap the player name at the top. Choose a player or enter a new name. Profiles and high scores stay on that device. Tap the trophy to see scores for each difficulty.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Gameplay, sounds, profiles, and scores work without an internet connection. The game has no ads, purchases, subscriptions, or account sign-in.

Sound and difficulty

Tap the speaker to turn sound on or off. Easy, Medium, Hard, and Impossible change how quickly the sequence plays. If you leave the app, tap Resume game to hear the full sequence again.
