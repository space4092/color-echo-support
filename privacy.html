COLOR ECHO

Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026. This policy applies to the Color Echo iPhone and iPad app, published by Lars Boesche.

Information in the app

Color Echo does not collect or transmit personal information, gameplay activity, player names, or scores to the developer or to a server. The app does not contain analytics, advertising, tracking tools, or third-party SDKs.

Player profiles and scores

You can play as Guest without entering a name. Optional player names and high scores are stored locally on your device, solely to show the selected player and keep scores for each difficulty. The app does not create an online account or synchronize these profiles between devices.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps local profiles and scores until you delete the app from your device. Removing the app deletes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore data included in that backup. The developer has no access to these records and cannot retrieve them or delete them remotely.

Permissions and sharing

The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising identifier. It does not share or sell user data. Sounds are generated on the device.

Support and external links

If you choose to open this support website or submit a support request on GitHub, your browser and GitHub handle that interaction under their own privacy practices. Information you choose to include in a public support request is visible publicly. Such information is not collected by the game.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Color Echo through the support tracker.
