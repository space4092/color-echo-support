COLOR ECHO

Privacy policy

Updated November 18, 2026. This policy applies to the Color Echo iPhone and iPad app, published by Lars Boesche. Version 1.2 and later, including TestFlight builds, add optional Game Center battles. Earlier versions provide local play only.

Local play

Solo play stores player profiles and scores on your device. Color Echo has no advertising, tracking or analytics tools, and the developer does not operate an account or gameplay server.

Player profiles and scores

You can play as Guest without entering a name. Optional player names and high scores are stored locally on your device, solely to show the selected player and keep scores for each difficulty. The app does not create an online account or synchronize these profiles between devices.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps local profiles and scores until you delete the app from your device. Removing the app deletes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore data included in that backup. The developer has no access to these records and cannot retrieve them or delete them remotely.

Optional Game Center battles

When you choose online play, Apple Game Center handles sign-in, invitations, connections and friend relationships under Apple's Game Center privacy information. Your Game Center nickname is visible to your opponent. The app temporarily uses game-scoped player identifiers, names, shared colour sequences, moves, timing and results to coordinate the live match through Game Center. These are not sent to a developer-operated server, used for advertising, or sold.

If you choose Load / refresh friends, Apple asks for permission to provide friends who also allow access and play Color Echo. You can deny access and still use Apple's invitation interface. The app holds this information in memory while running; it does not write friend lists, online identities or match histories to its local profile archive. Manage your Game Center account, friend permissions and Apple-held data in Apple's settings.

Permissions and sharing

Color Echo does not request direct access to your address book, location, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising identifier. Apple's friend-invitation interface may open Messages, where you choose the recipients and whether to send an invitation. Solo sounds are synthesized or played from bundled recordings on the device.

Support and external links

If you choose to open this support website or submit a support request on GitHub, your browser and GitHub handle that interaction under their own privacy practices. Information you choose to include in a public support request is visible publicly. Such information is not collected by the game.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Color Echo through the support tracker.
